## 🌟 Top Social Media Management Platforms Ecosystem

**A Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Content Scheduling, Multi-Platform Publishing, Analytics, Team Collaboration, Social Inbox & AI-Assisted Posting*

**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Social Media Management**. These tools help individuals, teams, and agencies plan, schedule, publish, monitor, and analyze content across major social networks from a unified dashboard.

**Examples** include Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social, Loomly, Later, Metricool, SocialPilot, Sendible, Agorapulse, Planable, and Publer (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted social media scheduling, multi-platform publishing, Fediverse management, and AI-assisted content tools — ideal for marketers, creators, agencies, and privacy-conscious teams seeking full control over their social data and workflows.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

| **[Agorapulse](https://www.agorapulse.com/)** | Social media management platform strong in social inbox, community management, reporting, and team workflows. | $79/user/mo | 1 owner/100 users, 50 profiles, 10 AI credits/mo | ~$18.0M |

## 💻 Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/n8n-io/n8n?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n/stargazers)  
  Open-source workflow automation platforms frequently used to build custom social media publishing, monitoring, and content pipelines.

- **[Postiz](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gitroomhq/postiz-app?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app/stargazers)  
  Leading open-source, self-hostable social media scheduling platform with AI features, broad platform support (including newer networks), team collaboration, and agent-friendly APIs.

- **[Mixpost](https://github.com/inovector/mixpost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/inovector/mixpost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/inovector/mixpost/stargazers)  
  Polished self-hosted social media management tool (Laravel-based) positioned as a Buffer alternative with clean UI and one-time or free core options.

- **[social-media-agent](https://github.com/langchain-ai/social-media-agent)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/langchain-ai/social-media-agent?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/langchain-ai/social-media-agent/stargazers)  
  An AI agent by LangChain designed to assist in managing social media posts and automated engagement.

- **[brightbean-studio](https://github.com/brightbeanxyz/brightbean-studio)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/brightbeanxyz/brightbean-studio?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/brightbeanxyz/brightbean-studio/stargazers)  
  Open-source multi-channel content management tool and scheduling platform.

- **[Socioboard](https://github.com/socioboard/Socioboard-5.0)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/socioboard/Socioboard-5.0?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/socioboard/Socioboard-5.0/stargazers)  
  Long-standing open-source social media management, analytics, and reporting platform supporting multiple networks and multi-tenant/agency use cases.

- **[TryPost](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/trypostit/trypost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost/stargazers)  
  Open-source social media scheduler with native publishing to many networks, calendar view, and AI copilot capabilities.

- **[Free-AI-Social-Media-Scheduler](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/Free-AI-Social-Media-Scheduler)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Anil-matcha/Free-AI-Social-Media-Scheduler?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/Free-AI-Social-Media-Scheduler/stargazers)  
  A lightweight AI-driven tool to schedule and automate social media workflows for free.

- **[MagicSync](https://github.com/leamsigc/MagicSync)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/leamsigc/MagicSync?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/leamsigc/MagicSync/stargazers)  
  Open-source social media and content management platform built with modern stacks, supporting multiple networks and AI content generation.

- **[SocialRing](https://github.com/sanjipun/socialring)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/sanjipun/socialring?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/sanjipun/socialring/stargazers)  
  Free and open-source social media management platform focused on posting to major networks with expanding feature set.

### 🌐 Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[FediSuite](https://www.fedisuite.com/)**  
  Open-source platform specialized in managing Fediverse accounts (Mastodon, Pixelfed, PeerTube, Misskey, and more) with scheduling, threading, and multi-account support.

- Odoo Social Marketing module and other ERP/CMS social add-ons.
- Custom scripts and bots using official platform APIs (with proper rate-limit and ToS compliance).
- Self-hosted analytics dashboards that pull data from open APIs or exports.
- AI content generation pipelines integrated with open schedulers.
- White-label and multi-tenant forks of the major open-source platforms for agencies.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Deploy **Postiz** or **Mixpost** for core scheduling and multi-platform publishing, extend with **n8n**/workflow tools for advanced automation, add Fediverse support via **FediSuite** or native integrations, and layer local LLMs for content ideation, hashtag suggestions, and performance insights while keeping all credentials and post history under your control.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Social media platforms frequently change their APIs, rate limits, and terms of service. Open-source tools require ongoing maintenance to stay compatible and compliant.
- Self-hosted solutions handling social credentials and content must implement proper security, access controls, and data protection practices. Always respect each network’s developer policies and user privacy requirements.
